ABOUT IGGEMA

Progress moves
faster together.

IGGEMA is a platform for the people and businesses shaping India's green gas future.

We aim to connect equipment manufacturers, technology providers, project developers, researchers, and policymakers working across biogas, BioCNG, biomass, green hydrogen, and waste-to-energy.

By creating space for shared knowledge and meaningful collaboration, we champion indigenous innovation and help accelerate India's shift to cleaner, more circular energy.
