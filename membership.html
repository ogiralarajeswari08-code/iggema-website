All members are expected to have read, understood, and accepted this Charter as a condition of their membership.

PART III

Member obligations & code of conduct

  1. Accuracy of Information — Members shall ensure that all information provided to IGGEMA — including company details, contact information, and project data — is accurate, current, and updated within 30 days of any change.
  2. Fee Payment — Annual membership fees shall be paid within 30 days of the renewal date. Non-payment beyond 60 days will result in suspension of membership benefits. Reinstatement will require payment of arrears plus the re-admission fee.
  3. Ethical Conduct — Members shall conduct their business with integrity, transparency, and in compliance with all applicable laws and regulations. Any conduct that brings IGGEMA or the sector into disrepute will be subject to disciplinary review by the Governing Council.
  4. Confidentiality — Members shall treat any non-public information shared within IGGEMA platforms, working groups, or member communications as confidential and shall not disclose such information to third parties without express written consent.
  5. Intellectual Property — IGGEMA’s name, logo, and branding may be used by members solely in the context of their membership designation (e.g. “IGGEMA Member”). Any other use requires prior written approval from IGGEMA.
  6. Participation — Members are encouraged to actively participate in IGGEMA events, surveys, working groups, and industry advocacy efforts. Active participation strengthens the collective voice of the sector.
  7. Reporting Misconduct — Members who become aware of any ethical violations, fraud, or misrepresentation by another member or party are encouraged to report the same to IGGEMA’s Governing Council in confidence.

PART IV

Grievance, suspension & termination

Any grievance by a member may be submitted in writing to the Secretary General at awards@iggema.org. The Governing Council shall review and respond within 21 working days. In cases of misconduct or non-compliance, the Governing Council reserves the right to suspend or terminate membership after providing the member with an opportunity to be heard. Fees paid are non-refundable upon voluntary termination or termination for cause.

PART V

Amendments to this Charter

IGGEMA reserves the right to amend this Charter, including fee schedules and benefit descriptions, with 60 days’ prior notice to all members via registered email. Continued membership after the notice period constitutes acceptance of the amended terms.
